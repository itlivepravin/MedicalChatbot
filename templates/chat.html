Medical Knowledge Assistant

Ask the documents, not the void.

This interface sends your question to the Flask app, retrieves relevant document chunks from Pinecone, and answers with the context stored for this project.

Good prompts Be specific. Ask about symptoms, treatments, side effects, dosage guidance, or definitions found in your uploaded PDFs.
Response style The backend is set to answer briefly and admit when the indexed documents do not contain the answer.
